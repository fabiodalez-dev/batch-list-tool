# How to protect your account with two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication adds a second check to your sign-in: in addition to your password, you enter a 6-digit code generated by an authenticator app on your phone. This guide explains how to turn it on from the Two-factor authentication page, how to store your recovery codes, and how to sign in once it is enabled.

## Prerequisites

- [ ] An active user account and your current password.
- [ ] An authenticator app installed on your phone, such as 1Password, Authy, Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator.
- [ ] A secure place to store your recovery codes, such as a password manager.

## 1. Open the Two-factor authentication page

Sign in with your email address and password, answer the security check question, and select **Sign in**. In the sidebar, select **Two-factor authentication** in the **My Account** group.

![Open the Two-factor authentication page](./block_open-two-factor-page.png)

## 2. Start the enrolment

Select **Enable two-factor authentication**. In the dialog that opens, select **Confirm** to generate the secret for your authenticator app.

## 3. Scan the QR code

Open your authenticator app, add a new account and scan the QR code shown on the page. If you cannot scan the code, select **Show setup URL** and enter the setup details in the app manually.

![Scan the QR code](./block_scan-qr-code.png)

## 4. Confirm the 6-digit code

Enter the 6-digit code that your authenticator app currently shows in the code field, then select **Confirm enrolment**. The status at the top of the page changes to confirm that two-factor authentication is enabled.

## 5. Store your recovery codes

Record the recovery codes listed in the highlighted panel before you leave the page, as this is the only time they are shown. Store them in a password manager, on paper, or anywhere else that only you can reach; each code can be used once if you lose access to your authenticator app.

## 6. Sign in with a code

The next time you sign in, enter your email address and password as usual. On the **Two-factor challenge** page, enter the current code from your app in **Authentication code** and select **Sign in**.

![Sign in with a code](./block_sign-in-with-code.png)

## If you lose your phone

On the **Two-factor challenge** page, select **Use a recovery code instead**, enter one of your unused codes in **Recovery code** and select **Sign in**. Once you are signed in, open the Two-factor authentication page and select **Regenerate recovery codes**, or select **Disable two-factor authentication** and enrol your new phone; both ask for your **Password** before they go ahead. If you have lost both your phone and your recovery codes, contact your administrator for assistance.

## Tips

> **Tip:** Authenticator codes change every 30 seconds. If a code is rejected, wait for the next one and enter it promptly.
>
> **Tip:** Two-factor authentication is strongly recommended for every account with the Administrator role.
>
> **Tip:** Regenerating your recovery codes immediately invalidates the previous set, so replace any stored copies at the same time. Recovery codes are shown only once, right after they are created: they cannot be displayed again later.

## Summary

Your account now requires a code from your authenticator app at every sign-in. Keep your recovery codes in a safe place, as they are the only way to sign in without your phone.
