# How to protect your account with two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication adds a second check to your sign-in to the Logistics Tool: in addition to your password, you enter a 6-digit code generated by an authenticator app on your phone. This guide explains how to sign in, how to turn on two-factor authentication from the **Two-factor authentication** page, how to sign in with a code afterwards, and what to do if you lose your phone.

## Prerequisites

- [ ] An active user account with your email address and password.
- [ ] An authenticator app installed on your phone, such as 1Password, Authy, Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator.
- [ ] A secure place to store your recovery codes, such as a password manager.

## 1. Sign in with your password

On the **Sign in** page, enter your **Email address** and **Password**, type the answer to the **Security check** question as a digit, and select **Sign in**. If you are signing in for the first time, the **Profile** page opens and asks you to change your password: enter it in **New password** and **Confirm new password**, enter your **Current password** and select **Save changes**.

## Password requirements

- At least 12 characters.
- At least one upper-case letter and one lower-case letter.
- At least one digit.
- The requirements apply whenever a password is set or changed; existing passwords remain valid until they are next changed.

## 2. Open the Two-factor authentication page

In the sidebar, under **My Account**, select **Two-factor authentication**. The page states that **Two-factor authentication is not yet enabled.**

![Open the Two-factor authentication page](./block_open-two-factor-page.png)

## 3. Start the enrolment

Select **Enable two-factor authentication**. In the dialog that opens, select **Confirm** to generate the secret for your authenticator app. Buttons that do not apply at the current stage are shown greyed out.

## 4. Scan the QR code

Open your authenticator app, add a new account and scan the QR code shown under **Step 1**. If you cannot scan the code, select **Show setup URL** and enter the setup details in the app manually.

![Scan the QR code](./block_scan-qr-code.png)

## 5. Confirm the 6-digit code

Under **Step 2**, type the code that your authenticator app currently displays and select **Confirm enrolment**. The status at the top of the page changes to **Two-factor authentication is ENABLED.**

## 6. Store your recovery codes

Record the recovery codes listed in the highlighted panel before you leave the page, as this is the only time they are shown. Store them in a password manager, on paper, or in another place that only you can reach; each code can be used once if you lose access to your authenticator app.

## 7. Sign in with a code

The next time you sign in, enter your email address, password and the answer to the security check as usual. On the **Two-factor challenge** page, enter the current code from your app in **Authentication code** and select **Sign in**.

![Sign in with a code](./block_sign-in-with-code.png)

## 8. If you lose your phone

On the **Two-factor challenge** page, select **Use a recovery code instead**, enter one of your unused codes in **Recovery code** and select **Sign in**. Once you are signed in, open the **Two-factor authentication** page and either select **Regenerate recovery codes** to replace your codes, or select **Disable two-factor authentication**, enter your **Password** and select **Confirm**, then enrol your new phone. If you have lost both your phone and your recovery codes, contact an administrator of the Logistics Tool for assistance.

## Tips

> **Tip:** Authenticator codes change every 30 seconds. If a code is rejected, wait for the next code and enter it promptly.
>
> **Tip:** After five unsuccessful attempts within a minute, sign-in is paused briefly. Wait a minute before trying again.
>
> **Tip:** If you do not select **Remember me** when you sign in, you are signed out automatically after 30 minutes without activity. Leave **Remember me** unselected on shared computers.
>
> **Tip:** Regenerating your recovery codes immediately invalidates the previous set, so replace any stored copies at the same time.
>
> **Tip:** Two-factor authentication is strongly recommended for every account with the Administrator role.

## Summary

Your account now requires a code from your authenticator app at every sign-in. Keep your recovery codes in a safe place, as they are the only way to sign in without your phone.
